Before you start.

What is the difference between this page and Start creating?

This is the Snipo Clips product homepage. Start creating opens our separate onboarding funnel, where you choose a video source before continuing to sign-in and the app.

Can I edit the AI-generated reels?

Yes. Open Your folders → Edit video to use the Video Studio, or Edit captions to correct the transcript and style it. Current Studio timelines support up to five minutes.

Where are my videos saved?

Generated clips and edited exports are stored in your account. Download videos you want to keep; media and drafts expire with their original clip after 30 days.

3. Third-party processors

We use trusted providers for AI processing, hosting, analytics, authentication (Google) and payments. They process data only on our instructions.

4. Your content & AI

Your videos are processed to generate clips. We do not use your private content to train third-party models without your consent.

5. Data retention

Uploaded media, generated clips and activity analytics are automatically removed from our active storage after 30 days. Account and billing records are retained separately while needed to provide your account and meet our obligations. Social connection details and encrypted tokens remain while connected; disconnecting deletes that connection and its Snipo Clips publication records. A Reel already published on YouTube, Instagram or Facebook remains there until you remove it on that platform.

YouTube API Services

When you connect a YouTube channel, Snipo Clips uses YouTube API Services. We request permission to read the channel identifier, title and thumbnail, and to upload clips you explicitly choose. We store those channel details and encrypted access and refresh tokens to keep the connection working, plus the upload status, chosen visibility and YouTube video ID. We use these details only to connect your channel and perform and show the uploads you request. Read the Google Privacy Policy for Google's data practices.

Disconnecting in Social accounts removes the connection and stored tokens from Snipo Clips. You can also revoke access from Google Account permissions; to remove an uploaded video, use YouTube Studio. Contact privacy@snipoclip.com to request deletion of stored YouTube upload records or other account data.

Meta connections

When you connect Instagram or a Facebook Page, we store its ID, display name, encrypted access token, connection status and the status of Reels you explicitly request. For a selected Reel, we provide Meta temporary access to that clip so it can process the upload. We do not collect your personal browsing history or screen time. Performance insights are unavailable until the necessary Meta permissions are approved.

Disconnect in Social accounts to delete the connection, token and Snipo Clips publication history, or revoke Snipo Clips in your Instagram or Facebook account settings. Removing a published Reel requires action on Instagram or Facebook. For further deletion requests, email privacy@snipoclip.com. See Meta's Privacy Policy for its own processing.
