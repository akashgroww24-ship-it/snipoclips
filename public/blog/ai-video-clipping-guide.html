From long video to short stories: your Snipo Clips workflow

Choose a source, guide the AI, review your highlights and finish every clip in your own style.

Snipo Clips guides · Updated 10 October 2026
IN THIS GUIDEStart with a source worth revisitingGive the AI a useful directionReview, personalize and keep your work

Start with a source worth revisiting

A useful short usually begins with a useful long video. Choose a podcast, interview, lesson or recording that contains complete ideas. Use footage you own or have permission to edit. In Snipo Clips, paste a supported YouTube link or choose a video file. A link that opens in your browser can still be unavailable to the importer because of access restrictions; uploading your own source file is another starting point.

The creation funnel lets you prepare your source and preferences before signing in. It does not upload a file, charge you or start AI processing. Once you reach the app, check the source, settings and available allowance before starting generation.

Give the AI a useful direction

Open Full control to choose your target clip length, requested number of clips, aspect ratio and spoken language. A creative brief can help highlight selection: try ‘Find three practical tips for first-time founders. Keep each clip focused on one idea with a clear opening.’ Treat the requested count as a target; your source, available highlights and plan allowance can limit the output.

Snipo transcribes the source, selects highlights and renders captioned clips. Follow the processing stages in the workspace. A finished clip can appear while other clips are still rendering. Review the video itself rather than treating an AI score as a promise of views.

Review, personalize and keep your work

Open the generated folder and review each clip for context, awkward openings and incomplete endings. Correct transcript mistakes in Edit captions. Use Edit video to open the Video Studio when you want to adjust cuts, framing, layers or audio. You decide what the final reel should look like.

Export and download a copy you want to keep. Media and editing drafts expire with their original clip after 30 days. Posting more consistently can help you learn what your audience responds to, but neither AI selection nor caption styling guarantees reach or earnings.

Ready to try it in Snipo Clips?

Prepare your source and settings, then continue to your workspace.
