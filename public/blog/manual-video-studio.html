Video Studio · 2 MIN READ

AI made the first cut. Here’s how to make the final cut yours.

Trim, split, reorder and add your own footage, text, images or music in the Video Studio.

Snipo Clips guides · Updated 10 October 2026
IN THIS GUIDEOpen the editor from a finished clipBuild a clearer storyExport deliberately

Open the editor from a finished clip

Find your generated folder in Your folders and choose Edit video on a finished clip. The Video Studio gives you a manual editing workspace after the automated first cut. Watch the clip once before editing and decide what you want to improve: a tighter opening, a cleaner ending, a different order or a stronger visual explanation.

Start with one change at a time. The current Studio supports timelines up to five minutes. Your original generated video stays intact while you build the edited version, so you can compare the new result with the initial cut.

Build a clearer story

Trim away unnecessary pauses at the beginning and end. Split a segment when you want to remove part of a thought, and reorder segments only when the story still makes sense. Adjust speed and framing to suit the footage. Automatic pause removal can be a starting point, but review the resulting cuts so important words and natural emphasis remain.

Add your own video, image or audio assets where they make the explanation clearer. Text and image layers can highlight a key idea; music can support the mood. Use assets you have rights to use, check the audio balance and avoid covering the speaker or captions with too many overlays.

Export deliberately

Review the entire timeline before exporting, including the transitions between segments. Check the opening, the final frame, caption readability and audio. Studio exports use your account’s plan: the current paid Studio output supports up to 1080p, while free output uses 720p with a watermark.

The exported video is saved with your account’s clip, and a Studio draft lets you return to the project. Media, uploaded editor assets and drafts expire with the original clip after 30 days. Download a copy of the final export you want to keep.
