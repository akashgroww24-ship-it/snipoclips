Keep your clips organized: folders, names and safe deletion

Know what each folder contains, rename it clearly and understand what happens when you delete it.

Snipo Clips guides · Updated 10 October 2026
IN THIS GUIDEThink of a folder as one creation sessionRename around your workflowDelete only after keeping what you need

Think of a folder as one creation session

In Snipo Clips, generated videos are grouped by their generation job. Your folders keeps those clips together, so you can return to one source or creation session without searching through unrelated videos. Open a folder to review the finished clips and choose the one you want to edit.

Use the Shorts and AI Reels categories to narrow your view. A descriptive folder name makes the collection easier to recognize later. Include something useful such as the topic, episode or intended audience instead of relying only on a date.

Rename around your workflow

Choose Rename on a folder card, enter the new name and save. Keep the name on one line and within 80 characters. Examples include ‘Founder interview — hiring tips’ or ‘Physics lesson — quick explanations.’ The saved name belongs to your account’s folder.

Before a new editing session, watch the clip and confirm that you are working with the right source. Open Edit captions for wording and visual caption changes, or Edit video for timeline work. A clear folder name helps you find those drafts again.

Delete only after keeping what you need

Choose Delete beside Rename, or use Delete folder while a folder is open. Read the confirmation first. Deleting a folder permanently removes its Snipo clips, caption edits, Studio drafts, uploaded editor assets and saved exports. Download any finished work you want to keep before confirming.

Deletion is blocked while generation or a related edit, export or publishing operation is in progress. It does not remove videos already published on social platforms and does not restore your plan allowance. The normal 30-day media retention still applies to folders you keep.
