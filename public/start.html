A few things to know.

Can I edit what AI creates?

Yes. The Video Studio supports cuts, rearranging segments, captions, text and image layers, imported footage, music, framing and speed. The current editor supports timelines up to five minutes.

When will I choose a paid plan?

You can start with the available free allowance. Review pricing whenever you need more usage. Your account’s plan and remaining allowance are checked before generation.

Where do my videos go?

Generated clips and editor exports are saved in your account’s cloud storage. Download videos you want to keep: media and editing drafts expire with their original clip after 30 days.

Will a link start processing automatically?

No. This page prepares your starting point. Sign in and review your video and settings before starting generation in the app.
